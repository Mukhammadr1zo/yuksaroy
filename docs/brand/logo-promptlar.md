# YukSaroy — logo prompti (AI o'zi improvizatsiya qiladi)

Bitta prompt, bitta xabar. Vosita loyiha tavsifidan kelib chiqib 4 ta bir-biridan farqli konseptni o'zi topadi; faqat ranglar berilgan, shakl va metafora aytilmagan.

```
Design a logo for "YukSaroy" and propose 4 completely different concepts. Do not ask me for a direction — read the project below and improvise: each concept must come from a different idea about what this company is.

THE PROJECT
YukSaroy is a digital logistics marketplace for Uzbekistan and Central Asia — one place where everything a cargo needs on its journey is found, priced and booked online. Today this world runs on phone calls, personal contacts and paper. YukSaroy brings it onto one platform:

- Terminals and infrastructure: railway freight terminals, private sidings (branch tracks of factories and logistics companies), customs warehouses, container yards, weighbridges, dry ports — a catalog and map of all of them with real capacity, working hours and transparent prices.
- Booking: online reservation of loading and unloading time windows for wagons and trucks, an instant price calculator, order status tracking from arrival to completion.
- Road transport: verified trucking companies and drivers for delivery from terminal to factory or warehouse ("last mile"), a truck-and-cargo exchange for empty return trips, roadside services for drivers (parking, food, rest).
- Assets and services marketplace: wagons for rent or sale, private locomotives, cranes and loaders, trucks, private sidings for lease, customs warehouses — owners list, customers request, the platform connects them.
- People: freight forwarders, customs declarants, logisticians offering their services, and job vacancies at terminals and logistics companies.
- Documents and paperwork: shipping documents, applications for shipper codes, acts, invoices — generated, signed and stored digitally.
- International reach: information and booking for the ports and corridors Uzbek cargo travels through — the Caspian, Black Sea, Persian Gulf, China — so an exporter plans the whole route from one screen.

The platform is multimodal: rail, road, warehouses and ports work together in it. Users are professionals who open it every working day: cargo owners and logistics managers of factories and trading companies, freight forwarders, customs declarants, trucking companies and drivers, terminal operators, owners of wagons, locomotives and sidings, warehouse operators. It is a serious B2B tool, not a consumer app.

THE NAME
"Yuk" means cargo, freight. "Saroy" means palace, and in "karvonsaroy" — caravanserai — it means the Silk Road inn where caravans of every kind stopped, traded, rested, changed transport and moved on. Uzbekistan is the historic heart of the Silk Road; YukSaroy is its digital caravanserai: the place where cargo, every kind of transport and every logistics service meet and trust each other.

WHAT THE BRAND SHOULD FEEL LIKE
Reliable, exact, calm, modern. Proud of Uzbek and Silk Road heritage without folklore or ornament for its own sake. Industrial and professional, but warm and human — a company that makes hard logistics work simple. It should stand next to the best fintech and logistics brands of the world and look like it belongs there, while being unmistakably from Central Asia. It should not be read as "railway only" or "trucking only" — it is the whole journey.

COLORS (use these, nothing else)
Deep navy #122A44 — primary, ink and dark backgrounds.
Teal #0E9384 — primary accent.
Amber #C77E1E — a single small warm accent, use sparingly.
Sand #F6F1E7 — light background.
White #FFFFFF.
Each concept may use two or three of these colors; each must also work in one color (navy only).

REQUIREMENTS
- 4 concepts, each built on a different idea, each with its own short name and a one-sentence rationale explaining what the mark says about YukSaroy.
- Each concept: an emblem that works alone, and a lockup with the wordmark "YukSaroy" (spelled exactly like this, title case, no other text).
- Flat vector logo style, clean geometry, must stay recognizable at 16 px and on a large banner; no photo, no 3D, no shadows, no gradients, no mockups.
- Present all 4 on one clean white sheet, each concept in a row: emblem in color, lockup, one-color version, app-icon version on navy.
- Avoid generic logistics clichés (globes, swooshes, arrows, shields, cartoon trains or trucks).
```
